🍗 RAID: Shadow Legends — Cascade Food Calculator

Calculates the TOTAL number of champions of each rank (starting from Rank 2) needed to cascade-feed your main champion, "food on food", from its current Rank up to the target Rank, plus the estimated energy cost. Rank 1 is not counted here as a starting point for the cascade — not because it "doesn't count", but because leveling Rank 1→2 is inefficient when you usually have plenty of 2★ champions straight from Shards.

Only count SPARE champions (besides the main champion you're ranking up) — i.e. duplicates you could feed. Reserves at a given rank reduce the need to grow food from scratch, and that reduction cascades down, lowering the need at lower ranks too.

This applies ONLY to the current rank — after ranking up, the level resets to 1 anyway, so at higher ranks we always calculate full leveling from scratch. Leave it at 1 if the champion is fresh at its current rank. Enter the level (visible in-game on the champion's card), and we'll calculate the remaining XP to max from the XP curve ourselves.

ℹ️ Super Raids do NOT affect these calculations — it's 2x energy for 2x XP (and 2x silver/loot), so the XP/energy ratio stays identical. You only gain time (fewer clicks), not energy efficiency.

How this is calculated (cascade logic)

  • To build ONE champion at Rank R, you need a champion at Rank R-1 (which gets promoted) + (R-1) champions at Rank R-1 as food.
  • So a total of R champions of rank R-1 "disappear" (1 gets promoted, the rest are eaten) to produce 1 champion of rank R.
  • We calculate TOP-DOWN: from the target Rank down to Rank 2, at each step subtracting the declared reserve, which cascades down and reduces the need at lower ranks.
  • The calculator also subtracts the "cost already paid" at your current Rank (since your champion is already there), so it only counts the ADDITIONAL food needed from now on.

Energy: only the champion that actually GETS PROMOTED at a given step (self) needs to be leveled to max — food can stay at level 1, regardless of whether it was freshly grown or came from your reserve (reserves only save the cost of GROWING, not the cost of leveling — since food never needs leveling).

XP/energy rate: ~1,080 XP/energy is your real measured rate from 9 full Brutal 12-3 runs (72 energy, no Super Raids) — the rate held steady in the 1079–1082 range throughout the whole sample. Super Raids don't affect this rate (see the note above).
